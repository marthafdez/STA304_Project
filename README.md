## Overview

This repository contains the project proposal and supporting files for our STA304H5F group project, **"Analyzing Factors Affecting Student Housing Choices."** The project investigates which factors matter most to students when choosing housing, and whether housing type and satisfaction differ across student groups.

## Research Questions

- **RQ1:** Which factors do students consider most important when choosing housing (e.g., distance to campus, rent, commute time, amenities, roommates)?
- **RQ2:** Does housing type (residence, off-campus, living with family) depend on international vs. domestic status?
- **RQ3:** Does housing satisfaction differ across housing types?

## Methodology

- **Target population:** Students enrolled in STA304H5 (Fall 2026)
- **Sampling method:** Stratified random sampling, stratified by housing arrangement (on-campus residence, off-campus rental, living with family)
- **Data collection:** Google Forms survey distributed to the target population
- **Simple test:** Correlation and simple regression (commute time vs. satisfaction)
- **Advanced test:** Multiple linear regression with model selection (satisfaction as outcome; housing arrangement, rent, commute time, and other ratings as predictors), including a VIF check
